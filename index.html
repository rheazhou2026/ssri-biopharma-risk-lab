06 / METHODOLOGY

Under the hood

View publication ↗
Target variable

Normalized abnormal return (NAR) from a short event window around a publicly announced alliance termination.

Regression

Ordinary least squares with interaction effects and a quadratic duration term. This explorer uses the reported final coefficients.

Sub-indices

Dependency and buffer use the paper's PCA-derived weighted components, scaled here by their respective weight sums for demonstrative 0–1 controls.

Limits

Original training records, normalization bounds, and fitted transformers were not supplied. The reported R² and AUC are study claims, not independently verified here.
